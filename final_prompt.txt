### The Problem: Safari Date Picker CSS Incompatibility

**Bug #1: `position: absolute` on `::-webkit-calendar-picker-indicator`**
- Location: Lines ~95-109 in `src/static/css/styles.css`
- Impact: Safari cannot handle absolute positioning on pseudo-elements
- Result: Calendar icon is misaligned or invisible
- Fix: Remove `position: absolute`, `right`, `top`, `transform` properties from the selector

**Bug #2: `display: none` in Safari-specific media query**
- Location: Lines ~113-119 in `src/static/css/styles.css`
- Media query: `@media not all and (min-resolution:.001dpcm)` (Safari-specific)
- Impact: Completely hides the calendar picker trigger in Safari
- Result: Safari users cannot open the date picker
- Fix: Remove entire Safari-specific media query block
